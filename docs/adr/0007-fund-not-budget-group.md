# Shared money is a Fund

The shared money ledger is called **Fund**, not Budget Group or “shared budget”. A Fund has members (People), optional Household link, its own Default Currency, Shopping Lists, Transactions, and **Rules** (ADR-0014)—not a separate Recurring Transaction entity. A Person's personal ledger remains **Budget**.

**Note**: “Recurring Transactions” wording in earlier drafts is obsolete.
