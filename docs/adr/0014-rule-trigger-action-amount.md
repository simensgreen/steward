# Rule: trigger, action, amount

Money automation is a **Rule** on a Budget or Fund, not a narrow “recurring transaction” type. A Rule has a trigger (inflow, outflow, or schedule), an **action** (Transfer or create Money Request), and an amount (percent or fixed). This covers payroll-like schedules, subscriptions, and percentage piggy-bank sweeps into another ledger. Supersedes the narrower framing in ADR-0010.
