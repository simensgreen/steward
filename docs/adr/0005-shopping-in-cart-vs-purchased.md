# Shopping: In Cart vs Purchased

A Shopping Item is not binary checked/unchecked. Steward distinguishes at least **In Cart** (picked up in the store, not finalized) from **Purchased** (bought, fully or in part). Purchased typically receives Stock and, when price is known, posts accounting on the list’s Budget or Fund (with Settlement Strategy attribution on Funds). This matches supermarket flow and avoids premature posts while still walking the aisle.
