# Budget detached from Household

Money is not owned by Household. A Person has a personal Budget; shared spending lives on a **Fund** (see ADR-0007). A Fund may optionally link to a Household, but need not—e.g. a trip fund without a shared pantry. Shopping Lists hang off Budget/Fund (ADR-0006), not Household. Stock and other domestic surfaces may still sit on Household—settling separately. Cross-links join the graphs; they do not merge ownership.

**Status**: accepted; Shopping ownership amended by ADR-0006.
